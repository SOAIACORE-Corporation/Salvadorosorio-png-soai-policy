# SOAiaCore · RADAR-CAL-01 Retrospective

Status: EXECUTED / DOCUMENTARY-RETROSPECTIVE / NOT BLINDED
Date: 2026-08-17

## Objective
Test whether the flexible psychosocial inference standard preserves analytically relevant contextual information that a rigid confirmation-only rule would tend to discard, without promoting mixed signals to facts.

## Reference corpus
Source: `Matriz Conductual y Psicosocial · v9.1 · Integración semántica`, sheet `F3 Validación 12`.

The reference contains 12 human-reviewed profiles and five domains per profile: conflict, care, desire, loyalty and repair. Total: 60 domain judgments.

## Operational proxy
**Rigid capture:** a domain is retained only when its adjudicated state begins with `Confirmado`.

**Flexible capture:** a domain remains analytically usable when the adjudication is contextual, mixed, focal, performative, affiliative or candidate; domains explicitly labeled `No confirmado`, `Sin evidencia suficiente` or `Sin evidencia retenida` remain excluded.

This proxy measures analytic retention, not truth or clinical validity.

## Result
- Rigid capture: **18/60 = 30.0%**
- Flexible analytic retention: **33/60 = 55.0%**
- Absolute gain: **+15 domains**
- Relative increase in analyzable coverage: **+83.3%** versus the rigid proxy

Largest profile-level gains occurred where the human adjudication itself was mixed/contextual rather than literal-confirmatory. For example, `auditorinternoacc` rises from 1 to 4 retained domains and `Angelo` from 0 to 3. Profiles already supported by direct confirmed evidence, such as Leo, Isaac Yahir and Victor Rodriguez Rodeguez, show no additional gain.

## Interpretation
The pilot supports the design claim that analytic flexibility increases sensitivity to mixed and contextual psychosocial patterns while preserving the original epistemic status of each signal. The new method adds most value where interactional meaning depends on context, performance, reciprocity, asymmetry, partial repair or affiliation rather than explicit statements.

## Limits
- This was not blinded; it reuses prior human adjudication.
- The rigid proxy is intentionally conservative and simplified.
- Primary microsequences were not independently re-read under both protocols.
- The result measures coverage, not global accuracy, prediction or diagnosis.

## Gate
**RADAR-CAL-01 Phase A: POSITIVE.**

Confidence: MEDIUM-HIGH for increased analytic coverage; MEDIUM for any claim of improved overall accuracy.

Next: Phase B should run both protocols independently on blinded primary microsequences and score pattern recall, false-certainty rate, contradiction sensitivity, inference reasonableness and confidence calibration.
